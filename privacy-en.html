Privacy Policy

Stenamo Market (formerly “Partenaire Dozie”) · Developer: Peter C.O. · Google Play app: com.partenaire.dozie · Effective 1 September 2026

Stenamo Market (“we”, “our” or “the app”) is an online marketplace that connects buyers with sellers (shops) in Cameroon. This policy explains what data we collect, why, how we store and protect it, and what rights you have over it.

💳 No payments in the app. In this version, no payment is taken inside Stenamo Market: payment happens in the shop, directly between buyer and seller. We therefore collect no payment data (bank card, mobile money, etc.).

1. Who we are

This policy covers the Stenamo Market application (Google Play ID com.partenaire.dozie, formerly published under the name “Partenaire Dozie”).

The data controller is Peter C.O. (Peter Okafor), the developer registered on Google Play under the name “Peter C.O.”, trading as a sole proprietorship in Douala, Cameroon. This is the same person who publishes the app on the Google Play Store and who operates the service. Full contact details are in section 13 (Contact).

2. Data we collect

2.1 Accounts and identity (buyers and sellers)

  • Full name
  • Phone number (used as the sign-in identifier)
  • Email address
  • Address (city / stated location)
  • PIN or password (stored only as a one-way hash; we never see the plain text)
  • Role (buyer or seller) and, where provided, a profile photo

2.2 Seller and shop data

  • Shop information (name, category, description, city)
  • Listings / products offered for sale: names, prices, descriptions, photos
  • Seller verification documents (evidence provided to verify identity or trading activity), stored securely and accessible only to the verification team

2.3 Transaction and messaging data

  • Orders (items, quantities, status, collection method)
  • Negotiation messages and counter-offers between buyer and seller
  • Reviews and ratings left by buyers
  • Content reports you submit (moderation)

2.4 Technical and device data

  • Device type, operating-system and app version
  • Push notification tokens (to send you notifications about your orders and messages)
  • IP address and error logs, for security and correct operation

2.5 What we do not collect

  • No payment data (payment happens in the shop)
  • We do not access your contacts, microphone or SMS
  • We do not request precise GPS location
  • No third-party advertising SDKs that profile users

3. How we use your data

  • Create and manage your buyer or seller account
  • Display shops and listings, and enable ordering
  • Enable negotiation, reviews and communication between buyers and sellers
  • Verify sellers and moderate reported content
  • Send you useful notifications (orders, messages)
  • Maintain security, and prevent fraud and abuse
  • Provide support when you contact us

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for targeted advertising and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Legal basis for processing

  • Contract: processing necessary to provide the service you signed up for.
  • Legal obligation: retaining certain data where the law requires it.
  • Legitimate interest: security, fraud prevention and content moderation.
  • Consent: for any optional feature that explicitly asks for it.

5. Sharing with third parties

We share data only with the infrastructure providers needed to run the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Render — application server hosting
  • Google Play — app distribution; we do not share your commercial data with Google
  • WhatsApp — only if you contact us through that channel (support, account deletion)

Each provider is required to use the data only to provide their service to us. No payment partner is used in this version.

6. Where your data is stored

Your data is hosted on our own infrastructure: a database managed by Supabase and an application server on Render. Some processing may take place outside Cameroon (data centres in the United States or the European Union) in the normal course of those providers' operations, which apply encryption at rest and in transit.

7. Retention

  • Data for an active account is kept for as long as the account remains open.
  • If you close your account, we delete or anonymise the data we are not legally required to keep, within 7 working days of your request (see the Account Deletion page).
  • Some data (for example, data needed to meet legal obligations) may be retained temporarily and then permanently deleted.

8. Your rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected
  • Delete your account and your data (subject to legal retention periods)
  • Withdraw consent previously given
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise these rights or delete your account, use the Contact section below or the Account Deletion page.

9. Security

  • HTTPS / TLS encryption for all network traffic
  • Hashing of passwords and PINs (never stored in plain text)
  • Token-based authentication with expiry
  • Access to verification documents limited to authorised staff
  • Row-level security policies on the database

No online service can be 100% secure. If a breach affects your data, we will inform you without undue delay.

10. Children

Stenamo Market is not intended for children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Cookies and local storage

The app uses the device's local storage to keep your session and your preferences. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top reflects the latest version; substantial changes will be announced in the app.
